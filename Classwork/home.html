About US

We are Group 7, a team of 100-level computer science students passionate about technology, innovation and problem-solving. As beginners in the world of computing, we are committed to learning, developing our skills and exploring how technology can solve real-world problems. Our goal is to grow into competent computer scientists and future technology professionals.
